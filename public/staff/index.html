    • Olga Pisarskiy
    • Owner and Head Coach

    • Olga has been coaching in the CSRA since moving to the area from Charlotte, NC in 2017. Her experience ranges from coaching Olympic Weightlifting in a class setting, group functional fitness, powerlifting, and a specialization in one-on-one Olympic Weightlifting coaching. Prior to opening a brick and mortar space, Olga was providing remote and in-person coaching as well as individualized programming. She has developed athletes and coached on all competitive levels from local meets to USAW National level events alongside her team; Butcher Barbell. Olga founded North Augusta Barbell after seeing a need for a dedicated Olympic Weightlifting space in the CSRA. Her passion is to grow the sport of Weightlifting and empower athletes to reach their full potential.

      Olga found the sport of Olympic Weightlifting through CrossFit in early 2016. She started competing in the sport shortly thereafter and has continued to compete as well as coach the past 10 years. Olga is married and has two middle school boys. Read more of her story HERE.

    • Harley Eades III
    • Coach

    • Harley is a USAW Level 1 coach passionate about helping individuals of all ages—from youth to masters—build strength, power, and conditioning. Whether you are discovering the sport of weightlifting or furthering your skills, their only coaching requirement is a commitment to putting in the work. Off the platform, they are a tenured professor at Augusta University with a PhD in Computer Science, where they research methods to improve the remote coaching of athletes.

      After loosing their athletic ability during college and graduate school Harley began focusing on his health and fitness in 2019. Starting by just walking and riding his bike he slowly lost weight, but during this process found CrossFit and then Weightlifting. Throughout the last six years he has lost over 100lbs, gained some muscle, and cultivated his love for weightlifting and helping others find the level of health and fitness they desire. Harley is a dedicated Masters athlete in weightlifting who puts in the same work as his athletes. He believes that through hard work and a passion for what we invest our time everyone can reach their goals.
